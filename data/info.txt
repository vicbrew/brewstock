section Date, Time and Location
Saturday 7 November 2026, from 2 pm until midnight.

The venue is a private property near Diamond Creek. The address is on your ticket. Please do not share the address on social media or websites.

Time will be available before the event for bump in and on Sunday for bump out. These arrangements will be communicated to the clubs, who will coordinate with their members

section Ticketing
Tickets are required to cover the cost of food, supplies and amenities. The ticket price does not cover alcohol. Alcohol is provided free of charge by the participating clubs and brewers

section Food
Ramblers American BBQ Food Truck will be serving up a taste of authentic American BBQ, with each guest receiving a delicious burger and a serve of smoky burnt ends during the event.

Delicious homemade sausages and extras will be available FOR SALE for those who are extra hungry.

Dietary requirements: We can only cater for vegetarian dietary requirements. Please indicate this at checkout. Unfortunately, if you have other dietary requirements, you will need to bring your own food. Please contact us for a discounted ticket.

Clubs running a themed bar have also been asked to incorporate snacks as part of their theme.

section Music & Entertainment
The event will feature:
- Live Music and DJ
- Break out sessions and presentation by sponsors
- Sponsor stalls
- Social activities
- Optional fun awards

The focus is on community and sharing great beer rather than competition.

The bands are generously playing without payment and would appreciate donations from anyone
enjoying the music. Please bring a little cash if you can to show your appreciation

section Responsible Service of Alcohol
Although Brewstock is a private event for club members, all participants are expected to support responsible service of alcohol.

Clubs should:
- Ensure all beverage servers hold a current RSA
- Serve small tasting pours rather than full pints (30-60ml)
- Comply with responsible service of alcohol laws, in regards to serving intoxicated, impaired or quarrelsome people
- Encourage attendees to pace themselves
- Promote water consumption and food availability

Water will be freely available at clearly marked locations throughout the venue, and attendees should be encouraged to make use of these facilities throughout the event.

PET glasses will be made available by the organising committee for all attendees. Clubs are encouraged to use tasting-sized pours to promote responsible consumption and allow attendees to sample beverages from multiple clubs.

Each club should ensure that those serving beverages are comfortable monitoring consumption and acting responsibly.

The goal is for everyone to enjoy the event in a safe and respectful environment.

section Transport
The venue is approximately a 10-minute drive from both Hurstbridge Station and Mernda Station.

Rideshare services are available from the stations; however, return rides later in the evening may take some time to arrive.

Clubs may wish to consider arranging maxi taxis or rideshare in advance to take attendees from the venue to either Hurstbridge Station or Mernda Station.

Those attending may want to look into accommodation for the night in Diamond Creek.

section Camping
There is plenty of space for tent camping on-site at this stage. However, spaces for caravans, campervans and similar camping vehicles are strictly limited due to space.

Everyone planning to camp must indicate this during the ticketing process, whether staying in a tent, caravan, campervan or another type of camping vehicle. Select the relevant camping option on the ticketing site after the payment screen.

section Theme
Each club has nominated a country and is already working on its theme. Clubs will decorate their bars, dress to suit their chosen country, and loosely align their beers and snacks with the theme.

If you have questions about your club’s theme, please talk to your club’s committee.
