About Brewstock

Brewstock is run by home brew clubs from across Victoria as a chance for members to come together, share what they have made, and learn from one another. Whether your passion is crafting hoppy ales, crisp ciders, full-bodied wines or fragrant meads, Brewstock is a relaxed, friendly day built around the things we love to make.

On Saturday 7 November 2026, brewers, makers and good company will gather to taste, talk and enjoy a program designed for the whole community. It is a celebration of local ingredients, practical know-how and the people who keep the tradition of home brewing alive.
